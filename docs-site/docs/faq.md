# Frequently Asked Questions

### Do you only build from scratch, or can you work on an existing codebase?

Both. A meaningful share of our work is picking up an existing project — fixing bugs, adding features, or improving test coverage and deployment on a codebase we didn't originally write.

### Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes a defined post-launch support window, and we offer ongoing retainers for maintenance, QA, or DevOps support beyond that.

### What if my requirements change partway through the project?

That's expected on most real projects. Anything beyond the originally agreed scope goes through a documented change-order process, so you always know the cost and timeline impact before it happens — no surprise invoices.

### Can you take over a project from a previous developer?

Yes. We'll typically start with a short paid assessment of the existing codebase before quoting the full engagement, since inherited code varies widely in condition.

### Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, as standard practice before any codebase or business data is shared with us — including when we bring in a specialist subcontractor for a specific stack.

### How do you handle payment for international clients?

We invoice directly and also work through Upwork and Fiverr, depending on where the engagement originates. Deposits are required on fixed-price work regardless of channel.
