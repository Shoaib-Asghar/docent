# How We Work

## Our engagement process

1. **Discovery call** — we understand the problem before proposing a solution
2. **Scoped proposal** — a written proposal or SOW covering deliverables, timeline, and cost, so expectations are aligned before any work starts
3. **Deposit** — 30–50% upfront on fixed-price engagements
4. **Small paid pilot** (new clients) — for larger engagements, we typically start with a small, well-defined task before committing to the full scope, so both sides can confirm fit before scaling up
5. **Delivery** — regular status updates, tracked against the agreed timeline
6. **Testing & handoff** — every deliverable ships with its test suite and deployment pipeline, documented, not left as tribal knowledge
7. **Support** — a defined post-launch support window, with retainer options for ongoing work

## What we ask of clients

- A single point of contact for decisions and feedback
- Timely feedback on scoped milestones — delays on the client side extend the timeline correspondingly
- Any change beyond the agreed scope goes through a documented change-order process, so both timeline and cost stay predictable
